## Won't have (this release)

Recorded so that the scope boundary is a decision rather than an omission.

- A graphical front end - the brief asks for backend functionality only.
- A database - persistence is a serialised text file.
- Automatically fixing or rewriting the analysed code; the tool reports only.
- Analysing languages other than Python.
- Syntactic analysis through the ast module - not taught in the module.
- Wrapping an existing linter such as pylint or flake8 - forbidden by the brief.
